          Depot

          Turtles take turns standing on the dock block to sort their items into three chests next to it and refuel. Keep the sky above the dock open.

          Each turtle flies at its own height, starting at the travel height. Pick one above your builds and protected areas. The junk chest fills fast with a big fleet, so feed it into a trash can or hopper line.

          Protection

          Turtles, computers and other ComputerCraft blocks are always protected.
